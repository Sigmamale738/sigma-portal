About Sigma eBooks

Our Vision

Sigma eBooks is a free and open-source platform designed to make reading accessible for everyone. Built on modern cloud technologies, it allows users to upload, share, and explore eBooks from anywhere in the world.

Our Story

This project began as a passion for knowledge and technology — combining both to empower independent readers and learners. It’s maintained by a global community of developers and readers who believe that education should be open and shared.

What We Offer

  • 📚 Free and open digital library
  • 🌐 Real-time cloud database access
  • 💬 Reader community & chat integration
  • 🔐 Safe login and data encryption
